# Practice from an approved concept: the Workbooks side of `flh_practice_review_feed_v1` (2026-11-19)

**PROTOTYPE.** It is private-workbench only and uses synthetic fixture state. It adds no route,
migration or provider call, and stores nothing. It is the Workbooks lane's answer to Extraction
2.0 item 134 (`flh_practice_review_feed_v1`, owner-review). It uses that lane's documented
contract read-only.

## What it reads

`web/src/prototype/extractionPracticeFixture.ts` is generated by
`scripts/validation/extraction_practice/build_fixture.py` from that lane's
`tools/extraction/fixtures/practice_v1/expected/`, and records each source file's SHA-256:

| File | Schema | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `07-workbooks-feed.json` | `flh_practice_review_feed_v1` | The Review Today row and the practice items with their recorded outcomes. It carries no keys, attempt texts or schedule |
| `packet-tutor__packet.json` | `flh_practice_packet_v1`, tutor mode, with rubrics (an explicit choice in that demo) | The answer keys, approved knowledge and quoted excerpts, shown only on an attempt or reveal |
| `packet-study__packet.json` and `.md` | `flh_practice_packet_v1`, study mode | The text copied for another agent. It has no answer keys and is byte-identical to `handoff-136/sample-packet/` |

**Checks on that data:**
- `practiceFeed.ts` checks every document at run time. It refuses:
  - another schema;
  - a feed item that carries an answer, rubric, due date or attempt text;
  - a reveal recorded as anything but `revealed`;
  - a feed and packets from different source revisions;
  - a study packet with rubrics.
- Grading mirrors that lane's `practice.grade()`. `grade_parity.py` runs the 15 shared cases in
  `grading_cases.json` through their Python, and `practiceFeed.test.ts` runs the same cases
  through the TypeScript.

## The path

**Review Today → Show the practice prototype (synthetic data)…** opens one row:
- `data-source="practice"`, reason `practice_outcome`, which is what the extraction lane proposed.
- The feed's recorded outcomes: 1 missed, 1 revealed, 1 self-checked, 2 correct.
- What it is based on: source `y-en-demo` revision 1 (`fd55a3600f1c`), the feed, tutor packet
  `pk-e6edb4e2ae2c`.

**Open the concept** then leads through five steps:
1. **Recall first.** The source is closed. An empty check says what to do and keeps focus on the
   field. Ctrl+Enter records a self-check and shows the reference. **Open the source now** counts
   as a reveal.
2. **Your source.**
   - Your quoted words, with the quote marked and its field, grounding, revision and `ev-` ID.
   - The approved knowledge with its origin. "Corrected by you" shows the earlier proposal beside
     it.
   - A generated example shows only its question until its exercises are answered or revealed.
3. **New sentences.** These are graded like the extraction lane:
   - an accepted variant is correct (case, punctuation and apostrophe forms ignored);
   - a known confusion is "not yet";
   - anything else is "check it yourself", because it may still be correct French.

   The key and excerpts appear only after the attempt. **Reveal without answering** is recorded
   as revealed.
4. **Choose the right form.** The answer must match an option exactly; anything else is "not one
   of the options".
5. **A study packet for another agent.**
   - **Copy the study packet** puts `pk-3cc014313f28` on the clipboard: no keys or rubrics, and
     never this visit's answers.
   - If the browser refuses, the text is shown and selected for Ctrl+C.

**← Back to Review Today** returns focus to **Open the concept**.

**State that disappears on reload,** each labelled where it shows: the panel's open state, every
answer, reveal and self-check, and the copy status. Nothing goes to browser storage; the tests
check that local and session storage are empty. Storing these is the decision in
`docs/plans/PRACTICE-STORAGE-DECISION.md`.

**Keyboard and narrow screens:**
- Every control is native.
- A row of buttons moves focus to each part's heading, and Tab then reaches its field.
- Ctrl+Enter checks the answer being typed.
- Focus moves to the concept heading on open and back to the opener on exit.
- At 320 px there is no horizontal scroll, and excerpts and the packet text wrap.

## Limits

- **One concept.** The fixture is one synthetic source; the panel lists what the feed holds.
- **Fixed outcomes.** The row's outcomes are the feed's recorded ones. This visit's answers do not
  change the row, and the page says so.
- **Packet is not regenerated.** The copied packet is the extraction lane's export. Exporting a
  new packet that includes this visit's misses is that lane's CLI (`practice export`).
- **French needs a check.** The French is synthetic A1/A2 and needs a native-speaker check
  before learner use, as that lane's handoff says.
