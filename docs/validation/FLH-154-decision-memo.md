## Results with uncertainty (item 34, synthetic)

Wilson 95 % intervals. The oracle's 1.0 rates mean nothing about a model: the
oracle is the reference.

| System | Frozen abstention (n = 20) | rb abstention (n = 20) | Paired consistency (n = 24) | Strict validity (n = 472) | Gates |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| oracle | 1.0 [0.839, 1.0] | 1.0 [0.839, 1.0] | 1.0 [0.862, 1.0] | 1.0 [0.992, 1.0] | pass |
| category-only | 1.0 [0.839, 1.0] | 0.0 [0.0, 0.161] | 0.0 [0.0, 0.138] | 1.0 | fail |
| constant-empty | 1.0 | 1.0 | 1.0 [0.862, 1.0] | 1.0 | fail (over-abstention 1.0 [0.979, 1.0]) |
| hallucinate-on-zero | 0.0 [0.0, 0.161] | 0.0 | 1.0 | 1.0 | fail |
| unsupported-swap | 1.0 | 1.0 | 1.0 | 1.0 | **pass** (heuristic F1 0.244) |

Probability that a system passes one gate, given its true rate (binomial at the
slice's sizes):

| Gate | true 0.80 | 0.85 | 0.90 | 0.95 | 0.99 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| abstention ≥ 0.90 (needs 18 of 20) | 0.206 | 0.405 | 0.677 | 0.925 | 0.999 |
| paired consistency ≥ 0.90 (22 of 24) | 0.115 | 0.280 | 0.564 | 0.884 | 0.998 |
| abstain-group accuracy ≥ 0.80 (8 of 9) | 0.436 | 0.599 | 0.775 | 0.929 | 0.997 |

Caveats:

- **Weak abstention can pass.** A system with a true abstention rate of 0.85
  passes one abstention gate 41 % of the time. One at exactly 0.90 passes both
  frozen and rb only ~46 % of the time, assuming the two parts are independent.
  They are not: they share every learner text. The gates separate clearly good
  from clearly bad systems, not neighbours.
- **Heuristic metrics are provisional.** Heuristic F1 and anchoring are
  provisional, and false claims are not measured at all.
- **The references are unreviewed.** Every reference is machine-authored and
  unreviewed; the reviewer sample is the first step.

  The renderer needs loopback for its in-process fake server.
- **Docker.** Only the in-image test run needs it (`TRAINING_RUNBOOK.md` §2).
